📋 Module 02

Bar Setup & Supplies

Set up the bar before doors open. Confirm you have the full list below.

Setup order

  1. Fold down 6 bags to use as ingredient bins — one per ingredient, arranged in the same order as the recipe card.
    • Use large serving bags for almonds, walnuts, and pumpkin seeds; use smaller bags for the dried apricots, dried blueberries, and dark chocolate chips.
  2. Pour the full supply of each ingredient into its bag right away. This order is sized for exactly 50 servings, so there's nothing held back to refill mid-event.
  3. Place one scoop in each bag — never let a scoop touch more than one ingredient; this protects guests with allergies from cross-contact.
  4. Stack extra smaller bags flat at the start of the line for guests to grab and fill as they move down the bar.
  5. Set up signage — the recipe card in its stand at the start of the line, and an ingredient benefit info card next to each bag so guests can see what they're adding.
  6. Food safe gloves are on hand for USW staff to use during station prep and setup, if needed — hand sanitizer is also provided if needed.
  7. Stage cleanup supplies — trash can and bags — within reach of the bar.
🛍️
Fold, don't tear

Fold the top of each bag down to create an open bin — never tear a bag open. Folded edges hold their shape and keep the bin sturdy through the event; torn bags collapse and look sloppy.

🥣 Module 03

Trail Mix Recipe & Ratios

The base recipe, scaled down — use it as the ratio guide for the full bar. This event's bulk order provides enough for 50 (3.5 oz) servings.

Ingredients

  • ½ cup unsweetened dried blueberries
  • ½ cup unsweetened dried apricots
  • 1 cup roasted almonds
  • 1 cup walnuts
  • ½ cup 70% dark chocolate chips
  • 1 cup pumpkin seeds (pepitas)
Yield: 18 servings (¼ cup each) 210 cal · 16g fat / serving

Instructions

  1. Portion into ¼-cup servings.
🥄
Scaling for the bar

This ratio is what the recipe card promises guests — roughly 2 parts fruit, 2 parts nuts/seeds, 1 part chocolate.

💬 Module 04

Event Talking Points

What's in the mix, why it matters, and how to start a conversation with guests at the bar.

🥄
Scaling for the bar

This ratio is what the recipe card promises guests — roughly 2 parts fruit, 2 parts nuts/seeds, 1 part chocolate.

Conversation starters

Use these to open a conversation while guests build their bag — no need to memorize a script, just pick what fits the moment.

  • "What's your go-to trail mix combo — more sweet or more salty?"
  • "Are you building this for a snack today, or saving some for later in the week?"
  • "Have you tried [ingredient] before? What do you usually pair it with?"
  • "What do you look for in a snack that keeps you going through a busy afternoon?"

Ingredient benefits

Unsweetened Dried Blueberries

  • Rich in antioxidants that help protect cells from oxidative stress, linked to a reduced risk of chronic conditions like heart disease
  • Supports cholesterol balance and helps protect brain cells and communication between them over time
  • Drying concentrates fiber, which supports digestion, gut health, and feeling full longer
  • Good source of vitamin C, K, and manganese

Unsweetened Dried Apricots

  • High in fiber — slows digestion, helps prevent blood sugar spikes, keeps you full longer
  • Rich in vitamin A/beta carotene, good for skin, eye, and immune health
  • High in potassium — helps maintain fluid balance and muscle function, great for guests planning to be active
  • Good source of iron, which supports energy levels and oxygen transport

Pumpkin Seeds (Pepitas)

  • High in plant protein — keeps you full longer, stabilizes energy, supports muscle repair
  • Great source of magnesium, supporting muscle function, energy production, and stress regulation
  • Good source of healthy unsaturated fats for long-lasting energy
  • Rich in zinc and iron, supporting immune function and energy production

Walnuts

  • High in omega-3 fatty acids (ALA), supporting heart health, brain function, and reduced inflammation
  • High in antioxidants and polyphenols that may help with memory, focus, and long-term brain health
  • Associated with lower LDL cholesterol and improved blood vessel function

Almonds

  • Good source of plant protein and monounsaturated fats — steady, long-lasting energy
  • Great source of vitamin E, supporting skin health and immune function
  • Good source of magnesium — great for active days, hiking, or workouts
  • Helps slow the absorption of sugars from the dried fruit in the mix

Dark Chocolate Chips (70% Cocoa)

  • Contains flavonoids that help protect cells from oxidative stress, support heart health, and reduce inflammation
  • Linked to improved blood flow, lower blood pressure, and better blood vessel function
  • May support mood by influencing brain chemicals like serotonin
  • Less sugar than typical chocolate candy — more cocoa means more beneficial compounds
  • 70% cocoa keeps sugar lower than a typical sweeter chip, without giving up flavor
📊
Nutrition facts, per ¼ cup serving

Calories: 210; Fat: 16g (primarily unsaturated, including omega-3 fats); Fiber: 3g; Protein: 6g

🥄 Module 05

Serving Guests & Station Flow

Keep the bar food-safe and welcoming through the whole event.

Running the bar

  • Confirm all 6 ingredient bags are stocked and in place before doors open, each with a matching ingredient benefit info card.
  • Glove up before handling ingredients directly.
  • Guests use the scoop provided in each bag — remind them not to use their hands.
  • Keep the stack of smaller bags topped off at the start of the line so guests always have one to fill.
  • Clean up any spilled ingredients right away and throw them away immediately — don't let them sit on the table.
  • Use the sanitation wipes provided to wipe down the table regularly throughout the shift.
  • Use the conversation starters in Module 04 to engage guests while they build their bag.
🧤
Food safety first
  • Never let a scoop cross between ingredient bags — one scoop per ingredient, always
  • Never touch ingredients with bare hands
  • Discard any ingredient that's dropped or spilled — don't return it to the bag
⚠️
Allergen awareness

This mix contains tree nuts (almonds, walnuts) and seeds (pumpkin seeds). Point out the ingredient info cards to any guest asking about allergens, and let them know exactly what's in each bag before they scoop.

🗑️
Trash management

Swap liners once ¾ full — never let trash overflow onto the table.

🌟 Module 06

Jordan's Wellness Program

Guests may ask about Jordan's free wellbeing program while they build their trail mix bag. Use this module to speak to it confidently and spark conversation — you don't need to be an expert, just curious and encouraging.

What the program offers

  • A free, self-paced wellbeing program powered by Personify Health, accessible at JordansFurniture.uswellness.com or through the Personify Health mobile app (iOS and Android).
  • Enrolling takes just a few minutes: create an account, accept consent, complete the Jordan's Wellness Program registration, and save. Access follows by email within 1 business day.
  • Once enrolled, employees can connect a fitness tracker or app — Garmin, Apple Watch, Fitbit, MyFitnessPal, and more — to earn credit for steps, sleep, and other activity.
  • Ongoing features include daily tips (Daily Cards), Healthy Habits, step and habit Challenges, a Media Library, Journeys® digital coaching (stress, nutrition, sleep, activity, and more), Social Groups, and Shoutouts to recognize coworkers.
  • Participants can "level up" as they engage with the platform, working toward a wellness incentive.

Conversation starters for guests

  • "Have you had a chance to check out Jordan's wellness program yet?"
  • "What's one health or wellness goal you're working on this year?"
  • "Do you already use a fitness tracker or app? Which one?"
  • "What would make it easier for you to build a new healthy habit?"
  • "Have you tried any of the challenges or Journeys in the app?"
  • "Is there a topic — like sleep, stress, or nutrition — you'd want more tips on?"

Where to send guests

  • Enroll: JordansFurniture.uswellness.com, or scan the QR code on the enrollment flyer
  • App: Personify Health mobile app, for iOS or Android
  • Support: support.personifyhealth.com or support@personifyhealth.com; for USW-specific help, askme@uswellness.com
💬
Keep it light

You're a conversation starter, not tech support. If a guest has an account issue, point them to askme@uswellness.com rather than troubleshooting at the bar.

If a guest has a question you can't answer on the spot, jot down their name, contact information, and question — the USW team will follow up with them directly.

📦 Module 07

Breakdown & Cleanup

End-of-event teardown — leave the bar ready to travel back to USW.

  1. Ask the HR team if they have a container to leave any extra trail mix behind for others to enjoy throughout the week.
  2. If HR has a container, transfer the remaining ingredients into it and leave it with them; if not, reseal the leftovers in their original bags.
  3. Package all reusable supplies — scoops, signage, extra individual portion bags, pop-up trash can, scissors, and hand sanitizer — to be returned to USW.
  4. Discard the used ingredient bags along with the rest of the trash, and tie it off.
